# TITONOMICS QC & Export

No QC pass = no delivery. Watch the full rendered video start-to-finish.

## 100-point rubric (≥90 to ship)
| Area | Pts | Pass test |
|---|---|---|
| Opening hook | 15 | Frame 1 stops scroll; mystery clear in 2s |
| Story & accuracy | 20 | Every scene supports narration; zero invented claims |
| Retention pacing | 15 | Visual change every 1–3s; no dead zones; loops cleanly |
| Visual quality & variety | 15 | ≥6 compositions; real footage first; no clichés |
| Captions & typography | 10 | Accurate, synced, mobile-readable, safe-zoned |
| Audio mix | 10 | Narration dominant; music secondary; no clipping |
| Mascot & branding | 5 | Original preserved; ~10%; varied placement |
| CTA & ending | 5 | Subtle, 55–70%, payoff-first ending |
| Technical export | 5 | 1080×1920, 30fps, H.264/AAC, valid MP4, full play |

## Auto-fail (fix + re-render)
Narration altered/cut • invented claim • watermarked/pirated footage • fake document/news as real • distorted mascot • unreadable captions • black bars/stretch • frozen/abrupt ending.

## Export spec
1080×1920 • 9:16 • 30 FPS • MP4 H.264 • AAC 48 kHz • high bitrate • no bars, no stretch, no watermark, no corrupted frames, no missing audio.

## Output
QC scorecard (per-area points + total), fix list, export file, delivery note (duration, story, CTA time, score, metadata, honest limitations). Never claim a check not performed.
