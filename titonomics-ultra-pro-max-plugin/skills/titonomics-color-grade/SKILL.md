# TITONOMICS Colour Grade

One coherent film from many sources.

## Palette
Deep navy, charcoal black, steel grey, restrained metallic gold, natural neutral whites.

## Grade rules
- Consistent exposure across all shots. Natural skin tones. Controlled highlights, visible shadow detail.
- Restrained saturation, sophisticated contrast, subtle colour separation. Natural whites.
- No crushed blacks, no excessive grain/noise, no artificial sharpening, no fake darkness.
- Each environment keeps believable natural colour while feeling like one film.

## Process
1. Match all footage to a reference (opening shot or mascot scene).
2. Apply grade, then check: phone brightness, shadows, skin, whites, gold restraint.
3. Reject any shot that looks like a different video.

## Output
Grade notes: LUT/parameters, per-shot adjustments, phone-view check result.
