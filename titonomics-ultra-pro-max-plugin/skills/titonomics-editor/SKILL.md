# TITONOMICS Editor

The MP3 is the timeline. Edit to the voice, not to a template.

## Cutting rules
- Cut on sentence boundaries, pauses, and emphasis. Use J/L-cuts, match cuts, motivated hard cuts.
- Transitions ≤6 frames unless a deliberate reveal. No transition may consume storytelling time.
- Punch-in 110–130% on emphasis words. Speed-ramp only on genuine action, never on evidence.
- Evidence stays ≥1.2s so numbers/labels are readable.
- No visual contradicts narration. No revelation before its spoken line. No delayed answer after it.

## Pacing
- Quicker cuts for info-heavy beats; longer shots for evidence and payoff. Never uniform speed.
- Visual change every 1–3s (see retention-director). No shot >3s unless key evidence.

## Output
EDL (edit decision list): timecode in/out, shot, transition, punch-in, caption cue, SFX cue per beat. Rough cut must play start-to-finish with complete narration.
