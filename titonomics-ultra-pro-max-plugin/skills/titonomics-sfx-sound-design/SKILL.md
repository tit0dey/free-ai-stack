# TITONOMICS Sound Design

Narration is king. Everything else serves it.

## Hierarchy
Narration (100%, dominant) > music (ducked −18 to −24 dB under narration) > SFX (accent only).

## Music
- Licensed, story-appropriate: restrained cinematic tension, subtle atmosphere, sophisticated documentary tone.
- No copyright risk. No sudden volume jumps. Fade in/out cleanly, never under the first/last word.

## SFX palette (use sparingly, one accent per reveal max)
Paper/document, mechanical hum, industrial ambience, subtle riser into revelation, soft impact on payoff. No whoosh spam, no bass-drop abuse, no repetitive impacts.

## Mix targets
- Integrated −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, no clipping, consistent loudness.
- Narration never masked, never faded, never cut off. Check on phone speakers + headphones.

## Output
Mix notes: music track + level, SFX cue list (timecode, sound, level), LUFS/peak readings, phone-speaker check result.
