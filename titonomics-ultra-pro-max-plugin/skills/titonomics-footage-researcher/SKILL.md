# TITONOMICS Footage Researcher

Real footage first. Graphics second. Decoration last.

## Target balance
~70% real cinematic footage / ~20% motion graphics & data / ~10% mascot.

## Sourcing rules
- Licensed, story-relevant footage only (factories, data centres, ports, banks, chips, infrastructure, documents). Verify rights; never watermarked/pirated/unclear.
- Never present AI/illustrative visuals as real news or official records. Never fabricate announcements, documents, or screenshots.
- Choose visuals by narration, not by what looks cinematic.

## Shot list (per beat)
`Beat | Primary shot | Backup shot | Graphic fallback | Purpose (context/subject/mechanism/evidence/tension/relationship/payoff)`

## Variety requirement
≥6 distinct compositions per Short: wide, medium, close-up, macro, overhead, graphic. Vary scale, movement, placement.

## Banned clichés
Neon glow, fake holograms, plastic AI rooms, handshake stock, staring-at-screens, repeated stock graphs, gold-coin rain, particle explosions, identical transitions, slideshow templates.
