# TITONOMICS Motion Graphics

Graphics explain. They never decorate.

## Suitable elements
Charts, geographic maps, corporate relationship diagrams, timelines, capital-flow visualisations, debt-structure illustrations, numerical reveals, institutional labels, document callouts, process visualisations.

## Accuracy rules (absolute)
- Only figures from the script. Preserve exact amounts, currencies, dates, units.
- No invented data points. No misleading scales. No invented corporate relationships.
- Illustrative graphics must never look like real financial records.

## Design rules
- Clean, mobile-readable, restrained. One idea per graphic. Max 2 simultaneous elements.
- Palette: deep navy, charcoal, steel grey, restrained gold, natural white.
- Animate only to reveal (draw-on, count-up, slide-in). No bouncing, no neon, no particle spam.
- Every graphic ≥1.2s on screen. Numbers exact and proofread.

## Output
Graphic spec per beat: type, data source (script line), animation, duration, safe-zone placement. Reject any graphic that duplicates what footage explains better.
