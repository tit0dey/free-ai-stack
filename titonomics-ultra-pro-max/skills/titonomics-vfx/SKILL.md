# TITONOMICS VFX

Invisible craft. If viewers notice the effect instead of the story, remove it.

## Allowed (budget: ≤2 noticeable effects per Short)
- Parallax / depth separation on stills and graphics.
- Whip-pan or graphic-match bridges between scenes.
- Subtle film grain, restrained light leak, gentle vignette.
- Punch-in emphasis, soft speed-ramp on action only.

## Forbidden
Neon glow, fake holograms, particle explosions, gold-coin rain, plastic AI environments, fake futuristic UI, identical transitions on every cut, excessive shake, oversharpening.

## Rules
- VFX must serve a beat (transition, reveal, emphasis). No decoration.
- Colour and lighting must match surrounding footage. No crushed blacks, no fake darkness.
- Every effect must be removable without breaking the story — if it is load-bearing, it is wrong.

## Output
VFX cue list: timecode, effect, purpose, intensity (low). Default to fewer effects; restraint is the style.
