# TITONOMICS Mascot Director

The mascot is the guide. The story is the star.

## Identity protection (absolute)
Never alter face, proportions, hairstyle, clothing, colours, or character identity. No redesign, no AI distortion. Original image only.

## Strategic roles
Document investigator, evidence pointer, detail inspector, revelation reactor, chart companion, transition bridge. Every appearance needs a story purpose.

## Placement & screen time
- ~10% of runtime. Varied positions (left/right/centre, foreground/background, full/half).
- Never permanent bottom-corner. Never over captions, numbers, or evidence. Never on every scene.
- Animation via controlled compositing (see 2d-chibi-animation). No distorted AI animation.

## Brand system
- TITONOMICS wordmark subtle and consistent. Palette matches grade (navy/charcoal/gold).
- No long outro, no logo screen. Branding supports the payoff, never replaces it.

## Output
Mascot plan: appearances (timecode, role, position, duration) + brand-check confirmation (identity intact, ~10%, varied).
