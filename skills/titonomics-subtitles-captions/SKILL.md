# TITONOMICS Subtitles & Captions

Readable at phone size. Synced to the audio, not guessed from the script.

## Rules
- Max 2 lines, ≤42 chars/line at 1080 wide. Phrase-level timing to actual MP3.
- One font family, two weights. High contrast, subtle shadow. No neon, no bouncing karaoke.
- Selective highlight (restrained gold) on names, numbers, key concepts only.
- Safe zones: central 70% vertically; avoid right 15% (controls) and bottom 20% (UI). Never hide captions under overlays.
- Proofread every caption against the MP3. Zero spelling errors, zero broken characters.

## Process
1. Transcribe/sync captions to MP3 sentence boundaries and pauses.
2. Break into phrases (not single words). Highlight sparingly.
3. Test at actual phone size; adjust size/position until readable in 1s.

## Output
Caption file (SRT/ASS or burned-in spec) + font/weight/size/position spec + proofread confirmation.
